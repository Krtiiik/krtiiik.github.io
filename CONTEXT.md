# Context

Glossary of the terms used in code, commits and conversation.

- **Hub**: the site itself. One home page that introduces me and links out to everything else.
- **Hero**: the top panel of the home page: name, intro, CTA and socials.
- **CTA**: the primary button in the hero (the résumé).
- **Card**: a tile under the hero linking one of my own sites or pages (Resume, Songbook).
- **Social**: an icon link in the hero to a profile elsewhere (GitHub, LinkedIn).
- **Panel**: the frosted box that pokes in from the left screen edge; used by the hero and content pages.
- **Scene**: one of the animated canvas backgrounds (DJ hardware, math, oscilloscope).
- **Scene dot**: the pulsing orange dot before the wordmark; clicking it switches the scene. Also the favicon.
